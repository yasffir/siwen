# Steady Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026. Version 1.

Steady is an app for habits, exercise, food, sleep, fasting, water and weight. This policy says what happens to what you put into it.

**In short**

- Without an account, what you log stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
- Progress photos never leave the iPhone you added them on.
- If you sign in, your data is also kept in your account on Steady's server in the EU, so it can be restored and is the same on your devices.
- The AI features are off until you turn them on. When they are on, what they need is sent to Anthropic to be analysed.
- Steady has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your data is never sold.
- You can export your data, sign out, or delete your account in the app at any time.

## What stays on your devices

Everything you log is stored in the app on your iPhone: habits and their check-offs, workouts, routes of outdoor workouts, meals and meal photos, sleep, fasts, water, weight, your profile (sex, height, year of birth, activity level) and your settings. The Apple Watch app exchanges workouts and habits with your iPhone directly.

Progress photos (photos of your body you choose from your photo library) are kept only on the iPhone you added them on. They are not part of your account, are not synced, are not in the backup file Steady exports, and are never sent to Steady's server or to anyone else. Where and with what camera a photo was taken is removed when you add it. "Erase all data" deletes them.

A file you import from another app (a CSV export) is read on your iPhone. The file itself is not sent anywhere; what you choose to import becomes entries like any others you log.

If you allow it, Steady reads from Apple Health: sleep, workouts with their routes and distances, active calories, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, VO₂ max, and breathing rate, blood oxygen and wrist temperature from your nights, and, for a habit you link to Health, its measure (steps, distance, flights climbed, active energy, exercise minutes or mindful minutes). Steady writes to Apple Health the cardio workouts you record, with distance, calories and route, and the meals you log, with their calories and nutrients.

Steady uses your location only during an outdoor workout you start, to measure distance, pace and the route. It uses the camera only when you photograph a meal or scan a barcode. Reminders are scheduled on the device.

If you never sign in and never turn on an AI feature, none of this is sent to Steady's server.

## What is sent when you sign in

An account is optional. If you make one, Steady keeps a copy of your data on its server so it can be restored and is the same on every device you sign in on.

**Your account.** Your email address, or the identifier and email address that Apple or Google gives Steady when you sign in with them. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email address. Steady never sees your Apple or Google password.

**Your data.** Habits and their check-offs; workouts with their exercises, sets, heart rate samples and routes; meals with their foods, nutrients and photos; sleep, fasts, water and weight entries; exercises and routines you made; your profile and your settings. This includes what Steady logged from Apple Health.

Only you can read your data: the server gives each account access to its own rows and files and to nothing else. It is used to store it and return it to your devices, and for nothing else.

Signing in is how you agree to this. Health data is kept in your account only because you agreed.

## AI features

Steady has three AI switches in Settings, all off until you turn them on:

- **Meal scans and coach.** Sends the meal photo or the description you typed. For the workout coach it sends your training goal, level, equipment, body weight and any limitations you entered, today's workout and cardio plan, this week's sets and your recent workouts.
- **Sleep and recovery analysis.** Sends daily summaries of your last three weeks: time asleep and awake, sleep stages, naps, your sleep quality score and how you rated waking up, bedtime and wake-up, heart rate variability, resting heart rate and what you trained; with your sleep goal, today's readiness score and what it is made of, and today's training plan.
- **Daily briefing.** Once a day, when you first open Steady, sends the time, last night's sleep and your sleep goal, today's workout and cardio plan and the workouts already done today, your fasting times, your calorie, protein and water goals, the names and reminder times of the habits due today, and yesterday's calories.

These go to Steady's server and from there to Anthropic, PBC (the maker of Claude) in the United States, which analyses them and returns the answer. Your name, email address and account are not sent to Anthropic. Anthropic states that it does not use data sent this way to train its models. Take care that a meal photo, a meal description or a habit's name holds nothing you would not want sent.

Workouts brought in from Strava are never part of what is sent.

Steady's server does not keep the photos, the descriptions, the workout details or the health summaries. It keeps exactly three things:

- the answer to a typed meal description, with nothing about who asked, so the same description can be answered again;
- a count of AI requests and food searches per day for each account or device, to limit cost and abuse (each person has a limit of AI requests a day, 25 at the time of writing);
- if you rate a meal scan with thumbs up or down, the rating, the meal's name and the list of foods the scan found (no photo), with your account's or device's identifier, to judge and improve the scans. With a thumbs down you can also say what was wrong (the calories, the amounts, the foods or the score, and which way) and add a few words of your own, up to 300 characters; those are kept with the rating.

With each AI request the app also sends the App Store's signed record of your Steady Pro subscription, so the server can check that the subscription is current. The record names the plan, its dates and transaction numbers; it holds no name, email address or payment details. The server checks it and does not keep it, and it is not passed on to Anthropic.

To use these features and the food search without an account, the app registers an anonymous identifier with the server. It carries no information about you, and is removed after 12 months without use.

Turn a switch off and nothing more is sent for that feature. The AI features are not offered to anyone under 16. AI answers are estimates, not medical or dietary advice.

## Strava

Connecting Strava is optional and separate from your Steady account. If you connect it (Settings › Strava), you sign in on Strava's own page and choose what Steady may do. Then, each with its own switch:

- **Send my workouts to Strava.** Workouts you record in Steady after connecting are uploaded to your Strava account: its name, the sport, start time, duration, distance, for an outdoor workout its route, and for a strength session its exercises with sets, reps and weights. Strava handles them under its own privacy policy and your Strava settings.
- **Bring in my Strava activities.** Steady reads your Strava activities of the last month and adds those it does not already have to your workout log: sport, start, duration, distance and, for rides, energy. [The app asks for `activity:read_all`, which includes activities you marked private on Strava. Decide whether to keep that.] They are shown as "From Strava". If you are signed in, they are kept in your Steady account with the rest of your log.

The keys Strava gives Steady stay in your iPhone's keychain. Steady's server passes your sign-in to Strava to exchange it for those keys and does not keep them or your Strava data. Activities from Strava are never sent to Anthropic or used by the AI features.

Disconnect in Settings › Strava, or erase all data: Steady's access is withdrawn on Strava and the keys are removed. [Workouts already brought in stay in your log until you delete them. Strava's API agreement may require deleting them on disconnect: decide before release.]

## Steady Pro

Steady Pro is a subscription bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment: Steady never sees your card, your name or your Apple Account.

- **On your iPhone**, the App Store tells Steady which plan you have, when it renews or ends, whether it is a free trial, and whether a payment is being retried. Steady uses this to unlock the Pro features and to show your plan in Settings. If you are on a free trial, Steady schedules a reminder on the device for the day before it ends.
- **If you are signed in when you subscribe**, the purchase carries your Steady account's identifier (a random code, not your email address), so Apple's records of the purchase can be matched to your account.
- **With each AI request**, the app sends the App Store's signed record of your subscription to Steady's server, as described under AI features. The server checks it and does not keep it.

Cancelling, refunds and the receipts themselves are handled by Apple, under Apple's own privacy policy.

## Food search and pictures

- **Searching for a food** sends the words you typed to Steady's server, which looks them up in the U.S. Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central. What is sent to the Department does not say who you are.
- **Scanning a barcode or searching packaged foods** sends the barcode or the words from your iPhone straight to Open Food Facts, a public food database. Like any website you visit, it sees your internet address.
- **Exercise pictures** are loaded from GitHub, which likewise sees your internet address.

## Legal grounds

- **When you delete your account:** your data, meal photos, ratings and request counts are erased from the server at once. Copies in the server's backups are gone within [N days; from your Supabase plan].

## Your choices and rights

In the app:

- **Export.** Settings exports your food, workouts and habits as spreadsheet files, and everything as one backup file.
- **Sign out.** Settings → Account → Sign out. Your data stays on the iPhone and in the account; nothing more is sent.
- **Delete your account.** Settings → Account → Delete account. Everything in the account is erased from the server. What is on your iPhone stays until you erase it there or remove the app.
- **Erase everything.** Settings → Erase all data removes your data from the iPhone and, if you are signed in, from your account and your other devices. It also disconnects Strava.
- **Strava.** Settings → Strava → Disconnect withdraws Steady's access to your Strava account.
- **Apple Health, location, camera, notifications.** Change what Steady may use in the iPhone's Settings.

Under the GDPR you also have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a form you can take elsewhere. Write to [email address]; you will get an answer within one month.

You can complain to your data protection authority. [In Luxembourg this is the Commission nationale pour la protection des données, cnpd.lu. Name the authority of the country you are established in.]
